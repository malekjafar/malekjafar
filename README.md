**Field Operations Reporting & Management Platform**

An operational reporting and management system designed to organize field-service data, workforce distribution, productivity, maintenance activities, CCT operations, reporting workflows, approvals, and operational outputs.

The system began as an advanced **Excel/VBA platform** and is being progressively rebuilt as a standalone desktop application using **Python and PySide6**.

**Key areas**

- Workforce and technician distribution
- Productivity and performance reporting
- Installation and maintenance operations
- CCT workflow management
- Data validation and processing
- Operational dashboards and KPIs
- Report generation and export
- Structured approval workflows

**Technology evolution**

`Excel` · `VBA` · `UserForms` ➜ `Python` · `PySide6` · `SQLite` · `SQLAlchemy`
